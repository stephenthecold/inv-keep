Users & roles

People who sign in via OIDC (OpenID Connect) appear here on first login, grouped under their role. Change someone's role with the Role dropdown, tick Lock to keep group mapping from overwriting it, or expand Permissions on a role to edit what it can do. Configure group→role mapping under Settings → Authentication.
